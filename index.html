Hit@1 — how often the message we wanted was the first result.

Example: Hit@5 of 85% means 85 out of 100 searches found the right message within the first 5 results. Higher is better.

How we tested: We used 32,245 messages from 448 Telegram channels and 10 discussion groups. We wrote 250 test searches, each in Uzbek Latin, Uzbek Cyrillic, Russian and English (1,000 searches total). Every model embedded all the messages and test searches, turning text into numbers to compare meaning. We checked where the expected matching message appeared in its results.
