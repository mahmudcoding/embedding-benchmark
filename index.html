Can an extra check improve the results?

How this was tested

Same test for every model. We searched 600 fictional Uzbek/Russian posts. The main accuracy score uses 32 test searches with known matching posts. The English test uses 500 documents and 50 searches from SciFact.

Computer. Apple M3 Mac with 16 GB of memory. Models ran one at a time. Qwen 4B and 8B used the smaller 4-bit versions shown in the table. E5 models split long texts into smaller pieces before encoding them.

Time. Total encoding time divided by the number of original texts: 600 for posts and 500 for English documents. This is an average from the batch run; a request sent on its own may take longer. Loading and warmup are excluded. Text length, other activity and your computer will affect speed.

RAM estimates. The two values marked ≈ come from a short check using three texts and the same model settings. Other values are the highest process RAM recorded during the original runs. Graphics memory may add to these figures.

Small test. A few searches can change the order. The fictional posts and expected answers have not had independent native-speaker review. These results do not establish accuracy on real Telegram data. The accuracy score also does not cover searches where no matching post exists.
